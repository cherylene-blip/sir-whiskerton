# Sir Whiskerton

A charming 3D cat musketeer you can chat with. Ask him for a backflip or his sword trick, or pay him a compliment. Open the page in Chrome or Edge on a computer and click **Wake him up**.

His AI brain (Qwen3 1.7B through [WebLLM](https://github.com/mlc-ai/web-llm)) runs entirely inside your own browser on your graphics chip. It's free and needs no account, and nothing you type leaves your computer. The first visit downloads the brain once (about 1 GB). After that it loads in seconds.

The whole page is the single file `index.html`, with the 3D model embedded.
